Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, 100%. Duckuu uses local `faster-whisper` for speech recognition and local Ollama (`qwen2.5:3b` / `llama3.2:3b`) for thinking. If you disconnect your Wi-Fi, every feature (voice dictation, PDF analysis, task management) continues working flawlessly on your local machine.
Duckuu renders directly onto an HTML5 canvas in a transparent, frameless Electron overlay. On mouse move, pixel alpha values are sampled (`ctx.getImageData > 10`). When hovering over transparent empty space, Electron sets `setIgnoreMouseEvents(true, { forward: true })`, allowing you to click icons and text behind Duckuu without interruption!
Unlike typical AI agents that execute arbitrary shell scripts without warning, Duckuu pauses all sensitive actions (opening apps, modifying files, broadcasting WhatsApp messages) on an internal `asyncio.Event`. An interactive card pops up in your Frosted Glass chat window waiting for your click ("Approve Once", "Always Allow", or "Deny"). Every decision is recorded into the SQLite audit log.
Duckuu requires Windows 10/11 (64-bit), 8GB of RAM (16GB recommended for local Ollama models), and approximately 1.5GB of disk space. For voice dictation, standard CPU inference executes in real-time with zero dedicated GPU required.
